Shader Development Journey

My journey with shader programs began in late 2014 through online tutorials. Initially, I found it frustrating until I discovered Shader Forge in 2016 and Shader Graph in 2018.

These node-based systems helped me visualize shader code and fundamentally understand shader creation. My transition back to Cg/HLSL started in late 2023, and I am currently focused on developing UI-friendly shaders.
